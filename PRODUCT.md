# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders, startups and business owners. Many are launching something new, or have outgrown a website that looks dated, breaks on phones or is hard to find. Their job on this site: decide quickly whether Webfluere is the partner who can design and build a credible website or app for them. Many arrive from Webfluere's Instagram reels and browse on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Webfluere is a digital agency that designs and builds websites and apps. A slow, broken or hard-to-find website costs a business trust and customers; Webfluere builds the version that works. The landing page succeeds when a convinced founder sends Webfluere a DM saying "BUILD" on Instagram.

## Positioning

The same team designs and builds. There is no hand-off between a design studio and a separate dev shop, so what gets designed is what ships. (Confirmed by the user, 2026-09-16.)

## Operating Context

- Traffic mostly comes from Instagram; every reel ends with the call to action DM "BUILD".
- The conversation and sale happen in Instagram DMs, not on the website.
- Scope right now is a single landing page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: websites that make a business look established and credible; responsive design for every phone, tablet and desktop; apps with smooth, easy-to-use experiences; SEO foundations (clean structure, fast pages, good technical setup, never promised rankings); smooth, friction-free UX.
- Verified numeric claims: "20+ projects shipped", plus the founder's Upwork record (see Evidence on Hand).
- Not provided, must not be invented: prices, team size, location, founding year, turnaround times, performance metrics, SEO ranking promises.
- Open: Instagram handle, email, phone and domain are not yet supplied. The site keeps visible placeholders until they are.
- Stack (existing repo): Vite, React 19.2, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion (framer-motion), React Three Fiber + drei. React stays on 19.2 because React Three Fiber does not support 19.3 yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Webfluere. One word, capital W only. Pronounced WEB-flu-AIR. "Fluere" is Latin for "to flow".
- Voice: genuine, sharp, approachable. Plain language, short sentences, no hype or buzzwords. Talk about what the customer gets, not about Webfluere. Example lines: "You bring the idea. We'll build the rest." / "Your website shouldn't feel like this."
- Logo: not decided. Use a text wordmark.
- The reel colors (#F3F2EE, #15161C, #3A2EFF) and Bricolage Grotesque are a working direction only; the user released the website from them (2026-09-15).
- User-pinned for the website (2026-09-16): a real 3D look built with React Three Fiber + drei, showing meaningful subjects such as devices and screens with work on them. Abstract blobs, liquid or amoeba-like shapes are rejected.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with real work. Never invent clients, numbers, quotes or results.
2. One path to action: DM "BUILD" on Instagram.
3. Design and build are one job. Show that what is designed is what ships.
4. A founder should understand the offer in seconds, in plain words.
