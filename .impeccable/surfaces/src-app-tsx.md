# Landing page (src/App.tsx)

Scope: the single Webfluere landing page, desktop and mobile. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: founders and business owners, many arriving from Instagram on a phone, deciding within seconds whether Webfluere can design and build their website or app.

Action: DM "BUILD" on Instagram, the only conversion path on the page. Handle still to be supplied; the page keeps a visible placeholder.

Proof: "20+ projects shipped"; six concept designs labelled as concepts; dedicated slots for real client projects and real testimonials that stay hidden until the user supplies material (no invented names, logos, quotes, or results).

Constraints: PRODUCT.md claims rules; user-pinned real 3D (React Three Fiber + drei) showing devices and screens, never abstract blobs; user-pinned premium scroll animation (2026-09-16: "I wanted the premium scroll animations and 3D websites"); offer and action visible in the first viewport without waiting on any reveal; reduced-motion users get a static page.

Memorable moment: scrolling pulls the laptop screen apart into sketch, design and build layers, then presses them back into one shipped site.

Unresolved: Instagram handle, email; real client projects and testimonials; logo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a mezzotint plate that one raking light burnishes as you scroll: Webfluere's work surfaces from velvet black, screen by screen, so a founder watches a finished product emerge from nothing. It refuses the dark agency hero with neon glow and floating mockups lit evenly from everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Rocked velvet warm-black ground #0b0a09 with a raster burr stipple, burnished silver (a brushed raster, never a CSS gradient) as the only light on controls, warm plate-brown only in half-scraped passages. One point light is the only light in the 3D stage: no ambient, no environment fill; screens surface through their burnish. Grotesk for headings and UI, engraved roman captions at 13px.

STORY: The visitor sees real work surface from darkness, watches design turn into a built site in the same hands, sees every service staged on real devices, turns through six labelled concepts, and sends DM "BUILD".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black plate. Left: headline, one-sentence offer and the burnished DM "BUILD" button, visible on load. Right on wide screens, above the copy on phones: a laptop, two phones and a browser window (cited: the page-wide stage reuses them in every chapter), all inside the frame, lit only by the raking light that follows the mouse and sweeps on its own on touch. Engraved plate caption in the lower margin, under the CTA on phones.

FORM: Mezzotint Velvet Night, a dealt challenger the user adopted over the assigned direction; outside my ordered list; seed key 4b647d7d. Signature interaction: one fixed React Three Fiber stage driven by pinned scroll chapters (story exploded view, five staged services, a turning ring of six concept plates).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
